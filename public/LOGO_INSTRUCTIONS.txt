Place your logo image here as:
- logo.png (recommended)
- logo.jpg
- logo.jpeg

Or in the images folder:
- images/logo.png
- images/logo.jpg

The invoice generator will automatically use the local logo instead of fetching from URL.

To download your current logo:
1. Go to: https://vaiyaaree.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/vaiyaaree_logo_round_small.png
2. Save it as "logo.png" in this folder (d:\aiswarya\public\)
3. Restart the server
